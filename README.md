# Northwind Customer Revenue & Retention Analysis

A business-focused data analysis project built on the Northwind dataset to identify **customer value, revenue concentration, retention risks, and operational performance**.

## Key Business Findings

* **1.27M** Total Sales
* **830** Customers
* **73.70%** of revenue comes from the Core Customer base
* **28.87K** Revenue at Risk
* **1.53K** Average Order Value
* **9.33** Average Orders per Customer
* Top customers show a strong concentration of revenue, making customer retention a key business priority.
