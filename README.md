A business-focused data analysis project built on the Northwind dataset to identify **customer value, revenue concentration, retention risks, and operational performance**.

## Key Business Findings

* **1.27M** Total Sales
* **830** Customers
* **73.70%** of revenue comes from the Core Customer base
* **28.87K** Revenue at Risk
* **1.53K** Average Order Value
* **9.33** Average Orders per Customer
* Top customers show a strong concentration of revenue, making customer retention a key business priority.

## What I Analyzed

* Customer Revenue & Value
* Customer Segmentation
* Revenue Concentration
* Customer Recency & Retention Risk
* Priority Customer Groups
* Shipping & Operational Performance
* Sales Trends
* Employee Sales Performance

## Tools

**SQL Server | Power BI | DAX | Data Modeling**

The analysis started with SQL-based data exploration and validation, followed by business-focused modeling and interactive Power BI dashboards.

## Business Goal

The objective was not simply to visualize the data, but to answer:

> **Which customers and business areas require attention, and where can the company take action to protect revenue and improve retention?**
